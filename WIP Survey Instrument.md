# **Survey Instrument**

**Title:** The Effect of Live-Streaming Commerce Exposure on Impulse Purchase Behavior Among Young Consumers in Ho Chi Minh City

**Design:** Two-wave panel (T1 baseline, T2 follow-up at 21 days post-T1)

**Target completion time:** T1 \~ 14-16 minutes; T2 \~ 12-15 minutes

**Mode:** Mobile-first online (Google Forms)

**Language:** Vietnamese (back-translated per Brislin, 1970\)

**Scale default:** 7-point Likert (1 \= Strongly Disagree, 7 \= Strongly Agree) unless otherwise specified

### **SECTION T1-E: BASELINE LIVE-STREAM EXPOSURE (6 items)**

*Instruction: "Thinking about the PAST 4 WEEKS, please answer the following about your live-stream shopping viewing habits."*

**Definition displayed to respondents:** A "live-stream shopping session" means a real-time broadcast on platforms like TikTok Shop, Shopee Live, or Lazada LazLive where a host demonstrates, reviews, or sells products. This does NOT include pre-recorded product videos, static product listings, or regular social media posts.

**EXP1.** In the past 4 weeks, approximately how many live-stream shopping sessions did you watch (even briefly)?

* *Numeric entry (0-999)*

**\[Note\]** EXP1 captures episodic recall count (absolute number of discrete viewing occasions). This item does NOT enter the composite index. It serves descriptive, cross-validation, and robustness purposes. Its measurement function is distinct from EXP5, which captures subjective frequency judgment.

**EXP2.** In the past 4 weeks, approximately how many hours total did you spend watching live-stream shopping sessions?

* *Numeric entry (0-200)*

**EXP3.** In the past 4 weeks, how often did you interact during live-stream sessions (e.g., posting comments, asking questions, clicking on products, tapping "like")?

* 1 \= Never  
* 2 \= Rarely (a few times total)  
* 3 \= Sometimes (once or twice per session)  
* 4 \= Often (multiple times per session)  
* 5 \= Very often (constantly during sessions)

**EXP4.** In the past 4 weeks, how many different live-stream hosts/sellers did you watch?

* *Numeric entry (0-99)*

**EXP5.** How frequently did you watch live-stream shopping sessions in the past 4 weeks?

* 1 \= Not at all  
* 2 \= Once or twice total  
* 3 \= About once a week  
* 4 \= Several times a week  
* 5 \= Daily or almost daily

**\[Note\]** EXP5 captures subjective frequency judgment (categorical self-assessment of viewing regularity). This item ENTERS the composite index (weight 0.40). Its measurement function is distinct from EXP1: EXP1 asks respondents to retrieve specific episodic memories and produce a count, while EXP5 asks for a global frequency evaluation. Subjective frequency judgments are more reliable than episode counts for retrospective self-report over multi-week windows.

**EXP6.** Compared to 3 months ago, my live-stream shopping viewing has:

* 1 \= Decreased a lot  
* 2 \= Decreased somewhat  
* 3 \= Stayed about the same  
* 4 \= Increased somewhat  
* 5 \= Increased a lot

**\[Note\]** Composite index specification: EXP5 (viewing frequency, weight 0.40), EXP2 (total duration, log-transformed, weight 0.30), EXP3 (interaction intensity, weight 0.30). All standardized to z-scores before weighting. EXP1, EXP4, EXP6 retained for descriptive analysis and robustness checks. See Researcher Codebook for full computation procedure.

### **SECTION T2-C: MEDIATOR \- PERCEIVED SCARCITY (5 items)**

*Instruction: "Thinking about the live-stream shopping sessions you watched since \[DATE\], please indicate how much you agree with each statement about what you perceived and felt DURING those sessions."*

# **Cookbook**

This section is ***not*** part of the respondent-facing instrument. It documents the analytical specifications, composite computations, flagging protocols, and variable-to-hypothesis mappings that support the Chapter 3 methodology and Chapter 4 analysis pipeline.

## **Composite Exposure Index Specification**

**Components:** EXP5/EXP5\_T2 (viewing frequency), EXP2/EXP2\_T2 (total duration), EXP3/EXP3\_T2 (interaction intensity)

**Computation procedure:**

1. Log-transform EXP2: duration\_ln \= ln(EXP2 \+ 1\)  
2. Standardize all three components to z-scores within each wave  
3. Apply weights: Exposure\_i \= 0.40 \* z(EXP5) \+ 0.30 \* z(duration\_ln) \+ 0.30 \* z(EXP3)  
4. The composite index is continuous; higher values indicate greater exposure intensity

**Weight justification:** Viewing frequency receives the highest weight (0.40) because it captures the regularity dimension of exposure, which is theoretically most proximal to habit formation and repeated stimulus contact. Duration and interaction receive equal weights (0.30 each) as complementary depth dimensions.

## **Primary DV Specification**

**Primary measure:** IPB1\_T2 (impulse purchase count at T2), analyzed via ANCOVA-framed regression controlling for IPB1 (baseline count at T1)

**Robustness checks:**

* Mean(IPB4\_T2, IPB5\_T2, IPB6\_T2): Likert composite perceptual frequency DV  
* IPB3\_T2: Ordinal/monetary sensitivity outcome (ordinal logistic or grouped OLS)  
* Poisson/negative binomial specification for IPB1\_T2 count data (if overdispersion warrants)

## **Mediation Analytical Roles**

* M1 (Perceived Scarcity): Mean(PS1\_T2 through PS5\_T2)  
* M2 (Parasocial Interaction): Mean(PSI1\_T2 through PSI5\_T2)  
* M3 (Entertainment Arousal): Mean(EA1\_T2 through EA4\_T2)  
* M4 (Social Proof): Mean(SP1\_T2 through SP4\_T2)

All mediators are measured at T2 only (concurrent with outcome). This is an acknowledged limitation per Imai et al. (2010) that constrains strict causal mediation claims.

## **Moderation Analytical Roles**

* W1 (Impulse Buying Tendency): Mean(IBT1 through IBT9, with IBT8 reverse-coded), measured at T1  
* W2 (Self-Control): Mean(SC1 through SC13, with reverse-coded items reversed), measured at T1

Moderators operate on b-paths (mediator \-\> outcome), not the direct exposure \-\> outcome path. This is moderated mediation, not simple moderation.

## **Lindell-Whitney CMV Adjustment**

**Marker items:** MV1\_T2, MV2\_T2

**Procedure:** Compute the smallest bivariate correlation (r\_m) between the marker item means and any focal construct composite at T2. Adjust all zero-order bivariate correlations among focal T2 constructs: r\_adj \= (r\_xy \- r\_m) / (1 \- r\_m). Test whether adjusted correlations remain significant.

## **Data Quality Flagging Protocol**

| Flag | Rule | Action |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| IMC1 fail | Response \!= 5 | Flag for review; exclude if fails 2+ checks |
| IMC2 fail | Response \> 3 | Flag for review; exclude if fails 2+ checks |
| CONSIST1 discrepancy | abs(CONSIST1 \- mean(IBT)) \> 3 | Flag for review |
| Implausible exposure (T1) | EXP2 \> EXP1 x 5 | Flag for manual review; do not auto-exclude |
| IMC1\_T2 fail | Response \!= 3 | Flag for review; exclude if fails 2+ checks |
| IMC2\_T2 discrepancy | abs(IMC2\_T2 \- mean(IPB4-6\_T2)) \> 3 | Flag for review |
| Implausible exposure (T2) | EXP2\_T2 \> EXP1\_T2 x 5 | Flag for manual review; do not auto-exclude |
| Completion time | Total time \< 5 minutes (T1) or \< 4 minutes (T2) | Flag as likely inattentive |
| Panel linkage mismatch | T2-A2 platform \!= T1-D1 platform AND code match is weak | Flag for verification |

## **Hypothesis-to-Item Mapping**

| Hypothesis | Path | Items Used |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| H1 (total effect) | X \-\> Y | Exposure composite (EXP5, EXP2, EXP3) \-\> IPB1\_T2, controlling for IPB1 |
| H2a (indirect via scarcity) | X \-\> M1 \-\> Y | Exposure \-\> PS1-PS5 \-\> IPB1\_T2 |
| H2b (indirect via PSI) | X \-\> M2 \-\> Y | Exposure \-\> PSI1-PSI5 \-\> IPB1\_T2 |
| H2c (indirect via arousal) | X \-\> M3 \-\> Y | Exposure \-\> EA1-EA4 \-\> IPB1\_T2 |
| H2d (indirect via social proof) | X \-\> M4 \-\> Y | Exposure \-\> SP1-SP4 \-\> IPB1\_T2 |
| H3a (moderated mediation, IBT) | W1 moderates M \-\> Y | IBT1-IBT9 moderates b-paths |
| H3b (moderated mediation, self-control) | W2 moderates M \-\> Y | SC1-SC13 moderates b-paths |
